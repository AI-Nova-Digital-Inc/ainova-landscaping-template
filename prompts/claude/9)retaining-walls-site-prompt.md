6. FAQ SECTION

Heading:
Frequently Asked Questions About Retaining Walls

FAQs:
Q1: Do I need a building permit for a retaining wall in Ontario?
A: Permit requirements depend on wall height, location, and municipality. In many Ontario cities, retaining walls over a certain height or close to property lines may require review. We help homeowners understand what applies to their project.

Q2: How long does a stone retaining wall last?
A: A properly built retaining wall with the right base, drainage, backfill, and materials can last for decades.

Q3: Why do retaining walls fail or lean over time?
A: Most failures happen because of poor drainage, shallow base preparation, weak backfill, or improper installation. Water pressure behind the wall is one of the biggest causes of movement.

Q4: Can you build steps into a retaining wall?
A: Yes. Retaining walls can be designed with integrated stone steps, landings, garden tiers, and walkways for both function and appearance.
