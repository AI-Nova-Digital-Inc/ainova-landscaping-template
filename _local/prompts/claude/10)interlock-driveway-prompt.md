Heading:
Our Interlock Driveway Installation Process

Description:
A beautiful driveway starts below the surface. Our installation process focuses on excavation, proper base preparation, drainage, screeding, paver alignment, compaction, polymeric sand, and optional sealing for long-term performance.

11 STEPS:

1. Preparation
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/01-preparation.jpg
Text:
We mark the driveway layout, confirm grades, review drainage direction, and prepare the site for clean excavation and installation.

2. Excavation
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/02-excavation.jpg
Text:
Old asphalt, concrete, soil, or unstable material is removed to the proper depth so the new driveway can be built on a strong foundation.

3. Base Preparation
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/03-base-prep.jpg
Text:
A compacted granular base is installed in layers to support vehicle loads and reduce movement through Ontario freeze-thaw cycles.

4. Screeding
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/04-screeding.jpg
Text:
A smooth bedding layer is carefully screeded to create an even surface for accurate paver placement and consistent final elevation.

5. Paver Installation
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/05-paver-installation.jpg
Text:
Pavers are installed in the selected pattern with clean alignment, proper spacing, and attention to the final visual finish.

6. Cutting
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/06-cutting.jpg
Text:
Pavers are carefully cut around borders, curves, edges, and fixed structures to create a clean professional finish.

7. Accent Borders
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/07-accent-borders.jpg
Text:
Contrasting borders and accent patterns are installed to define the driveway, add structure, and create a premium custom look.

8. Edge Restraints
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/08-edge-restraints.jpg
Text:
Edge restraints are installed to hold the pavers in place and help prevent spreading or shifting over time.

9. Compaction
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/09-compaction.jpg
Text:
The installed pavers are compacted into the bedding layer to lock the surface together and create a stable finish.

10. Polymeric Sand
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/10-polymeric-sand.jpg
Text:
Polymeric sand is swept into the joints and activated to help reduce weed growth, improve stability, and complete the surface.

11. Sealing
Image:
public/images/interlock-driveways/process/11-sealing.jpg
Text:
Optional sealing can enhance colour, protect the surface, and give the driveway a clean finished appearance.

Accordion style.

Heading:
Frequently Asked Questions About Interlock Driveways

Questions:

1. How long do interlock driveways last?
Answer:
With proper excavation, base preparation, drainage, compaction, and maintenance, a professionally installed interlock driveway can last for decades.

2. Will weeds grow between pavers?
Answer:
Polymeric sand helps reduce weed growth between pavers. Regular cleaning and maintenance also help keep joints looking clean.

3. Can interlock handle Ontario winters?
Answer:
Yes. Interlock is well suited for Ontario weather when installed with the correct base depth, compaction, and drainage system.

4. Does interlock sink over time?
Answer:
Sinking usually happens when the base is not properly prepared or compacted. A proper installation helps reduce settling and movement.

5. Is sealing necessary?
Answer:
Sealing is optional, but it can help enhance colour, reduce staining, and keep the driveway looking polished.
