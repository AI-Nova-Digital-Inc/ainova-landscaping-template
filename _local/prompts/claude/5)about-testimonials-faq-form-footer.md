Accent:

FAQ items:

1. Do I need a permit for a retaining wall in Kitchener or Waterloo?
   Answer:
   Permit requirements depend on wall height, location, and municipal rules. We help homeowners understand the project scope and recommend when engineering or permit review may be needed.

2. When is the best time for sod installation in the Waterloo Region?
   Answer:
   Spring and early fall are often ideal because temperatures are cooler and moisture levels are easier to manage. Summer installations can also work with proper watering and preparation.

3. What is the difference between interlock pavers and concrete driveways?
   Answer:
   Interlock pavers offer a premium look, flexible repair options, and strong curb appeal. Concrete can be simpler, but interlock usually provides more design options and easier maintenance over time.

4. How long does a typical patio or walkway project take?
   Answer:
   Project timelines depend on size, access, excavation needs, materials, and weather. Smaller projects may take a few days, while larger outdoor living spaces may take longer.

5. Do you provide free estimates?
   Answer:
   Yes. Everstone Outdoor Living provides clear, no-obligation estimates so homeowners understand the scope, options, and investment before work begins.
