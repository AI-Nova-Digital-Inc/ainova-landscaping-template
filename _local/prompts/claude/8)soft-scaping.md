5. FAQ SECTION

Heading:
Frequently Asked Questions About Softscaping

Use these FAQs:

Q1:
What is the difference between hardscaping and softscaping?

A1:
Hardscaping refers to the non-living elements of a landscape such as interlock patios, walkways, retaining walls, stone, concrete, and wood structures. Softscaping refers to the living and natural elements including trees, shrubs, perennials, sod, soil, mulch, and garden beds. A complete landscape usually blends both.

Q2:
When is the best time to plant trees and shrubs in Waterloo?

A2:
Early-to-mid spring and early fall are usually the best planting seasons in the Waterloo Region. The soil is workable, temperatures are moderate, and plants have time to establish roots before summer heat or winter freezing.

Q3:
How thick should mulch be applied in a garden bed?

A3:
For most garden beds, mulch should be applied around 2 to 3 inches deep. This helps suppress weeds, retain moisture, and regulate soil temperature. Mulch should not be piled against tree trunks or plant stems.

Q4:
Do you use native Ontario plants in your designs?

A4:
Yes. We often recommend native Ontario plants because they are adapted to local weather, soil conditions, and seasonal changes. They can require less maintenance and support pollinators such as bees and butterflies.
