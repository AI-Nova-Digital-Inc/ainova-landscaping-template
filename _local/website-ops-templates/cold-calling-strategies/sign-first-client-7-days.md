## Overview

**Strategy:** Offer free website mockup to cold leads → show them a professional AI-built version of their site → close for $1,000–$2,000+.

- Works anywhere in the world (creator signed client for $2,000 from Thailand)
- Complete beginner can execute — creator's friend closed a deal with zero prior experience
- Mockup build time: **under 5 minutes** using Reloom's URL import feature
- No money needed to start — collect payment before buying domain

## Step 1: Find Clients via Cold Calling

### Find Prospects on Google Maps

1. Google: `[industry] [city]` — e.g., `construction Toronto`
2. Click **Maps** tab
3. Scan listings — target businesses with **bad or outdated websites**
4. Avoid businesses with too many reviews (harder to reach owner)
5. Sweet spot: **69–120 reviews**
6. Grab their phone number from Google Maps listing → call

### What to Look For

- One-page websites
- Outdated design
- Missing key sections (no FAQ, no service pages, etc.)
- No website at all

---

## Cold Call Script

> "Hey, I just found you on Google. Great reviews, by the way. I clicked on your website and I noticed it could use some improvement. So I just went ahead and built you a completely new website that looks super professional. I was just wondering if you have maybe 5 to 10 minutes just to see it."

**If they have no website:**

> "Hey, I noticed you didn't have a website, so I just went ahead and built one from scratch — if you just have 5–10 minutes to see it."

**Offer framing:**

> "If they like it, it can be $997. If they don't, no worries — at least you know what a better website for your company would look like."

- Goal of cold call: book a **screen-share meeting**, not close the sale
- Full objection handling document available via DM on Instagram (message: "cold calling script")

**How the call went:**

## Step 2: Build the Mockup with Reloom (URL Import)

**Tool:** [reloom.io](https://reloom.io) — free tier: 1 project

### New Feature — Import from URL

Instead of manually describing the business, paste the client's existing website URL and Reloom scrapes all the information automatically.

**Process:**

1. Go to **reloom.io** → click **Launch**
2. Click **New Project**
3. Paste the client's website URL into the domain field
4. Click **Generate Sitemap** — AI reads their existing site and builds a full multi-page sitemap

**What Reloom adds automatically:**
- Expands a one-page site into multiple service pages
- Adds sections missing from their original site (e.g., FAQ section)
- Generates all copywriting from their existing content

**Selling point for FAQ section (use on closing call):**

> "Look at one of your competitors — they have an FAQ section. You don't. I'm sure you've had people call just to ask questions and then hang up without becoming a project. You're wasting your time. Every person who calls you should end up buying. The FAQ section handles those questions before they call."

### Edit the Sitemap

- Delete sections you don't want (e.g., Newsletter section)
- Add sections if needed
- Reorder sections by dragging the handle

### Generate Wireframe

- Click **Wireframe** → AI builds full page layouts with copywriting in under 1 minute
- Delete unwanted sections (e.g., Team section for small businesses)
- Reorder sections by dragging

## Step 3: Apply Style Guide & Branding

Click **Style Guide** → Reloom auto-applies:
- Brand colors
- Fonts
- Background images

### Add Client's Images

1. Go to client's existing website → right-click image → **Save Image As**
2. In Reloom Style Guide → click the image → upload saved file
3. Image swaps into the mockup

### Change Colors

**Option A — Surprise Me:**
- Click **Surprise Me** → AI picks new full color scheme (font + colors + branding)

**Option B — Manual brand colors:**
1. Use a color picker browser extension to sample colors from client's existing site
2. Copy the hex code
3. In Reloom → click color swatch → paste hex code
4. Colors now match the client's existing brand exactly

## Step 4: Create Multiple Concepts

> Reloom recommends at least **3 distinct concepts** before sharing with a client.

**Process:**
1. Current state = Concept 1
2. Click **New Concept** → click **Surprise Me** → new colors/fonts/branding = Concept 2
3. Repeat for Concept 3

- Each concept has different colors, fonts, and overall branding
- Present all three on the call → let client pick their favorite
- Higher perceived value — client feels they're getting options, not a template

## Step 5: Export to Webflow

**Why Webflow:** Produces a live, clickable preview the client can browse like a real website.

### Process

1. In Reloom → click **Webflow** export option
2. Click **Export** → follow steps to **Clone the Reloom Style Guide** into Webflow
3. In Webflow:
   - Go to **Apps** (left sidebar) → search **Reloom** → **Install on Site**
   - Plugin opens automatically → log in to Reloom
   - Select the project → choose which pages to import (e.g., Homepage only for initial call)
   - Select concept (e.g., Concept 1) → **Start Import**
4. Webflow builds the page — AI does it in front of you (saves ~7 hours of manual work per Reloom's counter)
5. Click **Preview Site** (top right) — fully interactive preview with animations
6. Share your screen on the call to walk client through it

### Publishing

- Click **Publish** → **Add Custom Domain**
- Buy domain on Squarespace or any registrar (~$15)
- **Do not buy domain before client pays** — collect money first, then buy domain with their payment

## Step 6: Close on the Call — Side-by-Side Comparison

On the screen-share closing call:

1. Open client's **old website** in one tab
2. Open your **new mockup** in another tab
3. Show both side by side
4. Walk through specific improvements:
   - More modern design / better hero section
   - Logos and layout properly centered and aligned
   - Multiple service pages (vs. one-pager)
   - FAQ section they don't currently have
   - Professional stock images (replace with their real photos before final delivery)
   - Consistent fonts, colors, copywriting

**Goal:** Make them feel the gap between where they are and where they could be.

## Critical Timing Rule

> **Do NOT build the mockup before the prospect confirms interest.**

- If you build before they confirm → they don't answer → 5 minutes wasted
- Correct sequence:
  1. Cold call
  2. They show interest + book meeting
  3. **Then** build mockup
  4. If they want it immediately: "Give me 10 minutes, I'll send you the link and hop on right away"
  5. Build in under 5 minutes → join call → present
