## Overview

- Goal: book 2–3 web design meetings per day via cold calling
- Strategy: tell prospects you already built them a website, ask for 5–10 minutes to show it
- Works even for complete beginners — follow the script, get meetings

---

## The Core Approach

1. Find businesses on Google with no website (or a weak one)
2. Call them — say you already built them a website for free
3. Ask for 5–10 minutes to show it (screen share / Google Meet)
4. Book the meeting — pitch price only on the meeting, not on the call
5. Before the meeting: import a template, slap their logo on it (takes ~5 minutes)

## Exact Cold Call Script

### Opening — Business Has No Website

> "Hey, is this [Business Name / Owner Name]?"

*(They confirm)*

> "Hey, I'm located in [City]. I just found you guys on Google — fantastic reviews by the way — and I noticed you didn't have a website, so I just went ahead and built you one from scratch. If you just have five to ten minutes to see it…"

---

### Opening — Missed Call Callback (They Called You Back)

> "Oh hey, how's it going? This is [Your Name]. I noticed you didn't have a website showing up online…"

*(If they do have a website — pivot immediately)*

> "Oh actually, I came across your website earlier and I noticed a few things that need to be improved, so I went ahead and built you a brand new website from scratch — completely free. It's already done. I just want to see if you have five to ten minutes to take a quick look at it."

---

### Opening — Business Has a Website (Needs Improvement)

> "Hey, I came across your website earlier and I noticed a few things that need to be improved, so I went ahead and built you a brand new website from scratch — completely free. It's already done. I just want to see if you have five to ten minutes today or tomorrow to take a quick look at what I've made for you."

## Handling Objections — Word for Word

### Objection: "I'm not really interested in a website right now."

> "I totally hear you — and most of my clients say the exact same thing before they actually see the website. It's actually able to bring them more clients. Like I said, it's already built. If you have five to ten minutes, even right now, I'll just share my screen and show it to you."

---

### Objection: "Can you just send me the link?"

> "I really wish I could do that, but it's actually hosted on my laptop since it's not published yet. So the only way we can do that is I just share my screen and show it to you. Even if you have five to ten minutes right now I could show it to you — or we could do it a bit later or another day."

---

### Objection: "What's the cost?"

> "It really does depend on the pages, but that's something we'll also be talking about as soon as you see the website. Anywhere from $200 to $5,000."

*(Don't get into pricing on the cold call — redirect to the meeting)*

---

### Prospect Is Busy Right Now

> "No worries at all — just let me know what day you're free and the time, and I can book it here on my calendar."

---

## Booking the Meeting — Closing Lines

### Get the Day and Time

> "What day works best for you — Tuesday or Wednesday?"

*(They pick a day)*

> "What time are you thinking — morning or afternoon?"

*(They give a time)*

> "Perfect. What email would be the best to send you the Google Meet link to?"

---

### Confirm and Anchor the Commitment

> "Just a heads up — I've spent quite a bit of time working on this website, so I just want to confirm that you're sure you can make it at [time] so we don't waste each other's time."

*(This reduces no-shows — makes them commit verbally)*

> "Perfect. I'll send you the invite. Looking forward to showing it to you!"

## Live Call Breakdowns

### Call 1 — Landscaping Company (No Website)

**What worked:**
- Opened with location + Google + "fantastic reviews" → instant rapport
- Said website is already built → lowered resistance
- When they asked to send a link: explained it's on laptop, not published yet → handled smoothly
- Let the prospect set the time ("15 minutes") → booked on the spot

**Key moment:**
> Prospect: "You already built the website for me?"
> Response: "Yep, already fully built. I'll just show it to you. If you like it we can publish it, if you don't I'll go kick rocks."

---

### Call 2 — Roofing Company (Weak Website)

**What worked:**
- Mentioned "a few things that need to be improved" → created curiosity
- Didn't flinch when asked about price — gave a range and redirected to meeting
- Got the day, time, and phone number to confirm

**Key moment — handling the price question:**
> Prospect: "What's the cost of that?"
> Response: "It really depends on the pages — that's something we'll talk about as soon as you see the website. $200 to $5,000."

---

### Call 3 — Inbound (Missed Call Callback)

**Situation:** Prospect called back after missing a call — Johnny didn't know who it was, answered anyway.

**How it was handled:**
- Quickly recovered: "I noticed you didn't have a website showing up online…"
- Pivoted mid-call when he realized they did have a website: "Actually I came across your website and noticed a few things that need to be improved…"
- Booked Tuesday 1pm despite initial confusion

**Lesson:** Even awkward calls can convert. Stay calm, pivot, keep pushing toward booking.

## Key Principles

| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| Website already built | Always say it's done — lowers resistance, creates curiosity |
| Never send a link | "Hosted on my laptop" — forces a live meeting |
| Don't pitch price on call | Redirect to meeting — "we'll cover that when you see the website" |
| Pick specific days | "Tuesday or Wednesday?" — don't say "whenever you're free" |
| Anchor the commitment | Confirm time + "don't want to waste each other's time" |
| Works for beginners | Script is simple — follow it, get meetings |

---

## Summary

- **Opening:** Found you on Google, great reviews, no website (or site needs work), already built you one for free
- **Resistance:** "Most clients say the same thing before they see it" — keep pushing gently
- **Link request:** Can't send link, not published yet, needs screen share
- **Price question:** Give range, redirect to meeting
- **Booking:** Pick day → pick time → get email → confirm commitment
- **Before the meeting:** Import template, add their logo — takes 5 minutes
- **On the meeting:** Show the site, give value, then pitch price and close
