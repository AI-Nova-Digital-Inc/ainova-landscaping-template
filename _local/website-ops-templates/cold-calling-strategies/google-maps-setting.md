Open:


```text

```

Sign in using your business Google account.

Recommended:


Use your real incorporated business name:

# STEP 4 — IMPORTANT PART (Hide Address)

Google will ask:

```text
Do you want to add a location customers can visit?
```

Choose:
```text
NO
```

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP.

Choosing NO means:
- Google verifies your address privately
- BUT does NOT show it publicly

This converts your business into:
```text
Service Area Business
```
