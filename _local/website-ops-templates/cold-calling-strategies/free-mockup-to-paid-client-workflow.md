## Overview

**Core strategy:** Offer a free website mockup to cold leads → get them interested → close them on a fully published website for $1,000–$2,000+.

- **Mockup build time:** ~5 minutes per client using AI tools
- **Full customization (with Figma):** 20–30 minutes
- **Close price:** $1,500 example shown in video
- **Tools:** Reloom (free), Figma (optional), Webflow (optional)
- **Lead source:** Cold Instagram DMs or cold calling

---

## Step 1: Get Leads via Cold Instagram DMs

### Find Prospects

1. Open Instagram → tap the **Search** button
2. Search: `[niche] [city]` — e.g., `landscaping Miami`
3. Browse posts → click any post → **open in new tab**
4. Open **~20 tabs** at once (one per prospect)

### Warm Up Each Account

For each prospect account:
1. **Like 3 of their posts**
2. **Follow them**
3. **Send a message** — phrase it like a curious potential client (not a salesperson)

### Opening Message Template

> "Hey, I just found your profile and love your work — do you work in all of Miami?"

- Avoid spelling mistakes
- Goal: get them to reply so you move from the **message request folder** into their **main inbox**
- Repeat for all 20 accounts

## Step 2: Pitch Web Design Services

Once they reply, send a **voice message** (converts better than text).

### Voice Message Script — Client Has a Website

> "Hey, I just found your profile and your work looks amazing. I slid up and I noticed your website could use some improvement. No worries — I already have it fully built out for you. I just want to show it to you if you have time for a 10–15 minute call."

### Voice Message Script — Client Has No Website

> "Hey, I just found your profile and your work looks amazing. I slid up and tried to click a link in your bio but I couldn't find a website. I was curious if you ever thought about getting a website. No worries — I already have it fully built out for you. I just want to show it to you if you have time for a 10–15 minute call."

### After They Show Interest

- Send your **phone number** so they can text you directly
- Book the call
- Then go build the mockup (next step)

## Step 3: Build the Mockup with Reloom

**Tool:** [reloom.io](https://reloom.io) — free tier available (1 free project)

### Process

1. Go to **reloom.io** → click **Start Free** (or **Launch** if already logged in)
2. Click **New Project** (top right)
3. Describe the company in a few words:
   - Example: `Abu Landscaping is a landscape company in Miami`
4. Click **Generate Sitemap** → AI builds the full site structure
5. Click **Wireframe** → AI generates the full page wireframe with copywriting

**Time to complete:** Under 2 minutes for the initial wireframe

---

## Step 4: Customize the Mockup

### Removing Unwanted Sections

- Click on any section (e.g., Team section)
- Right-click → **Delete**, or press **Delete** on keyboard

### Changing a Hero or Section Style

- Click the section → click **Shuffle Component**
- AI redesigns that section with a new layout

### Changing Colors

- Click **Surprise Me** → AI picks a new full color scheme
- Or manually select colors in the **Body** panel

### Changing Fonts

- Go to **Body** → **Font**
- Search font name (e.g., "Poppins") → select → preview updates instantly

### Adding Client Images

- Download client's images from their Instagram
- In Figma (after export): double-click image placeholder → swap in downloaded image

### Pitch Concepts Feature

- Click **Pitch Concepts** (top right of Reloom)
- Create multiple design variations
- Each concept gets its own link to share with client
- Client sees multiple options → higher perceived value

## Step 5: Share with Client

### Option A — Share Directly from Reloom (Fastest)

1. In Reloom → **Pitch Concepts** → **Copy Link**
2. Send link to client
3. Client opens it and browses all concepts like a real website

### Option B — Export to Webflow → Share Live Preview

1. In Reloom → top right → **Export** → select **Webflow**
2. Clone the Reloom style guide into Webflow
3. In Webflow: go to **Apps** → search **Reloom** → **Install on Site**
4. Log in to Reloom within Webflow
5. Select your project/concept → **Import All Pages**
6. Webflow AI builds out all pages automatically
7. Click **Share** (top right) → **Copy Read-Only Link**
8. Send link to client — they can click through it like a live website

**To publish with a real domain:**
- Click **Publish** in Webflow → **Add Custom Domain** → buy domain in Webflow

## Step 6: Export to Figma (Optional — Full Customization)

Use Figma for deeper visual customization before presenting to client.

### Setup (One-Time)

1. In Reloom → top right → **Export** → select **Figma**
2. Click both export option checkboxes → click **Export**
3. Follow steps to install the **Reloom Figma plugin**
4. Open the **Reloom Figma Kit** file

### Import Wireframe into Figma

1. In Figma → top right → **Plugins** → click **Reloom**
2. Log in to Reloom
3. Select the wireframe project → **Start Import**
4. Reloom imports all pages into Figma automatically

> Reloom reports time saved: ~2 hours 15 minutes vs. building manually

### Customize in Figma

1. Open a **new Figma file**
2. Copy wireframe from the imported file → paste into new file
3. Remove unwanted elements (e.g., mega menu): double-click to select → delete
4. Add client images:
   - Source from **Unsplash** or download from client's Instagram
   - Double-click image placeholder in Figma → replace with downloaded image
5. Swap in client's logo the same way
6. Adjust copywriting, FAQ, and brand colors to match client

**Total Figma customization time:** 20–30 minutes per client

---

## Step 7: Export to Webflow (Optional — Live Site)

If the client wants a live, hosted website on Webflow (beyond just a mockup):

1. Follow the Webflow export steps in [Step 5 Option B](#option-b--export-to-webflow--share-live-preview)
2. Connect a custom domain → **Publish**
3. This becomes the delivered final product the client pays for
