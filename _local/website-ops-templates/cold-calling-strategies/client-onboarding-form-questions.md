# Client Onboarding Form — Questions

*Source: form.html (JotForm — "Website Onboarding Form by Toure Marketing")*
*Use this to build the Tally form. Tally template to start from: search "Client Onboarding" in Tally gallery.*

---

## Section 1 — Your Details

1. **Name of Your Business**

2. **Company Owner Name** *(First Name / Last Name)*

3. **Personal Email** *(owner's personal email)*

4. **Personal Number** *(owner's personal phone)*

5. **Business Email** *(the email shown to customers)*

6. **Business Number** *(the phone number shown to customers)*

---

## Section 2 — Understanding Your Company and What You Offer

7. **Describe your business — company history and background.**
   *Hint: This helps us write a well-articulated About section.*

8. **Why should someone choose you?**
   *Think: Price / Quality / Care / Local / Honest / 5-Star Reviews / Guarantees / Accreditations / Ratings — the more the better.*

9. **Your brand's special sauce — what makes your company special or unique?**
   *Hint: WHY are you different from every other company in your area?*

10. **What services do you offer?**
    *Give a few examples.*

11. **Do you have any special offers, discounts, or finance/credit options?**
    *Example: Financing as low as $97/month. List anything.*

12. **Do you have any trust badges we can include on the site?**
    *Examples: Google Reviews, Facebook Reviews, BBB Rating, Angie's List, Yelp, manufacturer logos, Money-Back Guarantee badge, Quality Guarantee badge, etc. List as many as you can — we'll find the graphics.*

13. **Features and benefits — give visitors a reason to choose you.**
    *Examples: 24/7 Service, Free Inspection, Best Price Guarantee, Licensed & Insured, etc.*

14. **What does your customer journey look like?**
    *Example: They call or fill a form → Inspection → Estimate → Build → Follow-up*

15. **Preferred working areas and locations (up to 20)**
    *Cities, towns, neighbourhoods — as many as possible. These will be used for SEO.*

16. **Key messages — your vision, your values, your tagline.**
    *What do you want your ideal clients to know about you?*

17. **2–3 sentences on the history of the company.**
    *Feel free to include founding dates, milestones, etc.*

18. **Key competitors — list a few.**
    *Your closest local competitors by name.*

19. **Owners / Partners and their vision.**
    *Name each owner and include a short write-up: who they are, their background, and their vision for the company.*

20. **Upload: Owner / Partner photos** *(for the About Us section)*
    *Clear photos — include branded material if possible (logo on shirt, branded vehicle, etc.). Crew/team photos optional but welcome.*

21. **Upload: Website banner / hero image**
    *A good half-body photo of the owner or team works great. Send as many as you want — we'll use the best ones.*

22. **Do you have frequently asked questions (and answers)?**
    *List any FAQs you already answer for customers. We'll build an FAQ section from these.*

---

## Section 3 — Account & Login Details

23. **Website address** *(if you already have one — e.g. yourcompany.com)*

24. **Website admin login**
    - Username
    - Password / Login Code

25. **Additional login info or notes** *(optional)*
    *Hosting provider, registrar, anything else we need access to.*

---

*Note: Allocate 10–20 minutes to complete this form thoroughly. The more detail you provide, the better your website will be.*
