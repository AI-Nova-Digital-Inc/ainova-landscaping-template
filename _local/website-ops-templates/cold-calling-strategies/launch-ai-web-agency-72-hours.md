## Business Model Overview

- Core entry product: AI-built websites for local businesses
- Website is **transactional** — client pays once, gets immediate deliverable
- Real goal: use website as trust-builder → upsell **recurring services** (maintenance, hosting, marketing/ads)
- No web design experience required — AI handles mockup, Fiverr handles build
- Presenter claim: $18,000–$19,000 revenue within 10 days

> "A website is very transactional. We're using that to get into the door and make them trust us, and then upsell them on passive recurring services like maintenance, hosting, marketing."

## Phase 1 — Get the Best Clients

Three things required to scale:
1. **High-ticket niche** — clients who make a lot per job
2. **Eyes on you** — client acquisition methods
3. **Good offer** — irresistible, value-stacked pitch

### Pick a High-Ticket Niche

High-ticket niche = businesses where one job = thousands of dollars in revenue.

**Good niches:**
- Construction
- Home remodeling / kitchen remodeling
- Plumbers
- Electricians
- Concrete / hardscaping

**Why it matters — example comparison:**

| Client Type | Profit per job | Website cost | Jobs needed to break even |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant | $10/meal | $1,000 | 100 customers |
| Construction co. | $40,000/job | $5,000 | 1 job |

> "If you sell a construction company a website for $5,000, and they bring in one client, that $5,000 is much more worth it for that construction company owner."

High-ticket clients will pay more, see ROI faster, and won't haggle over price.

### Client Acquisition — Cold Instagram DMs

1. Search Instagram: `[niche] [city]` — e.g., `Toronto remodeling`
2. Open individual business profiles (not large brands)
3. Follow them, send opener DM:
   > "Hey, I just saw your profile and loved your work. Do you work in all of [city]?"
4. Goal: get them to reply → land in main inbox (not message requests)
5. Once they reply → send pitch via **voice message**

**Pitch script — no website:**
> "I was just checking out your profile and saw your work. It looks amazing. I tried to click a link in your bio and noticed you didn't have a website. To be honest, I thought it was a shame that you had such good work but didn't have anywhere to display it. So I went ahead and built you a new website. If you want to see it, it'll only take a few minutes."

**Pitch script — has a website:**
> "When I clicked on your bio, I noticed your website could use improvement. I thought it was a shame, so I just went ahead and made you a new professional website that looks 10 times better. If you want to see it, it only takes 5 minutes."

### Client Acquisition — Cold Calling

1. Google: `[city] [niche]` — e.g., `Toronto kitchen remodeling`
2. Click **Maps** — see all local businesses
3. Click through listings — most have bad or no websites
4. Call them using the same "already built" hook (see below)

### The "Already Built" Hook

**Key differentiator** from every other agency:

Other agencies say: *"Hey, I can make you a website. Are you interested?"*

You say: **"I already built you a website. I just want to show it to you. It'll only take 5 minutes."**

> "Worst-case scenario, you know what a new website would look like, and you have nothing to lose."

- Removes all friction and risk for the prospect
- Use AI to build the mockup in 5 minutes after they say yes
- Works for both cold DMs and cold calls

## Phase 2 — Build an Irresistible Offer

Goal: make the offer so good the client feels stupid to refuse.

### Value Stacking on the Sales Call

On the sales call, share your screen and walk through everything you're delivering:

1. High-converting website (already built)
2. SEO setup — organic traffic to the site
3. Contact form → leads go straight to client's email
4. Google My Business setup — appear in local search results
5. Optional: Google Ads setup for paid traffic

> "The client is going to brace himself for a big price like $5,000–$7,000. When you tell him it's only $2,000, he's like 'Wow, all this for only $2,000? Let's get started right away.'"

Stack the value high → anchor the perceived price high → reveal a lower price → instant yes.

## Phase 4 — Service Delivery

### Hosting Setup with Cloudways

**Platform:** [Cloudways](https://cloudways.com) — recommended hosting

**Why Cloudways:**
- $14/month flat — host **unlimited websites** on one server
- Charged $0.50/day (daily billing, not upfront)
- 24/7 live human support (even at 3am)
- Each client gets their own server (not shared hosting)
- Use **DigitalOcean** as server type (recommended)

**Setup process:**
1. Log into Cloudways → Deploy Application
2. Select **WordPress** as application type
3. Name the application after the client (e.g., `John Smith`)
4. Name the server by client location (e.g., `USA Clients`)
5. Select server size: **1 GB** (sufficient to start)
6. Select server location matching client's geography
7. Click **Launch Now**
8. Once launched → click the application → click **Admin Panel**
9. Copy username/password from Cloudways → paste into WordPress login
10. You're now inside the WordPress dashboard

> "WordPress is completely free. You do NOT go to wordpress.com. You use a hosting platform and access WordPress from there."

**Profit math:**
- 5 clients × $50/month hosting = $250 revenue
- Cloudways cost: $14/month
- Net: ~$236/month from hosting alone

### WordPress Access for Web Designer

Once you hire a web designer, give them access:

1. WordPress Dashboard → **Users** → **Add User**
2. Enter designer's name, email
3. Set role: **Administrator**
4. Copy the generated password
5. Send designer: (a) the WordPress admin URL (`yoursite.com/wp-admin`) + (b) the password via WhatsApp or message

### Hiring a Web Designer on Fiverr

1. Go to fiverr.com
2. Search: **WordPress Elementor**
3. Filter by relevant niche/location
4. Find designers building landing pages for $85–$150

**Economics:**
- You sell website for $500 minimum
- Fiverr designer costs $85
- Profit margin: ~80%+

### Agency Owner vs Freelancer Mindset

> "You don't want to be a web designer. You want to be a web design agency owner."

**Freelancer problems:**
- Handles client acquisition
- Handles client communication
- Builds every website themselves
- Handles upsells
- Only 24 hours in a day → stuck at $5k/month ceiling

**Agency owner approach:**
- AI builds the mockup (5 min)
- Fiverr designer builds the final site
- You focus on sales and client relationships
- Delegate → scale beyond your personal time

> "The people making the most money are the people working less. Agency owners at masterminds making hundreds of thousands per month are working 30 minutes to 1 hour a day."

## Phase 5 — Build Recurring Revenue

### Upsell Timing

Do NOT pitch additional services immediately after closing.

**Timeline:**
1. Close client → deliver website
2. Wait **1–2 weeks**
3. Check in: *"Hey, how did you like the website overall?"*
4. Client responds positively (praises work, shows family, etc.)
5. **Now** pitch the upsell — client is at emotional high

> "You want to sell them at a high. Once they're praising your website, that's when you pitch."

### Google Ads as Recurring Service

**How to pitch:**
> "Your website is already high-converting. All we need to do is run some Google Ads. We'll put a couple hundred dollars in, you'll get leads, they fill out the contact form, it goes straight to your email. You know how to close them from there."

**Pricing range:**
- Start: $500/month for ads management
- Scale: $2,000–$2,500/month (experienced agencies)

**Minimum ad spend requirement:**
- Tell client: minimum ~$30/day in ad spend
- Without minimum spend → no real results → unhappy client

**If you can't run ads yourself:**
- Outsource to a trusted partner
- Example: pay partner $500/month per client, charge client $750–$1,500
- Margin matters less than client results and relationship

> "I don't care how much money I lose. I want to provide my clients the best results possible because satisfied clients give referrals."

### Referrals

- Ask for referrals **2 weeks after** delivering results (not right away)
- Wait until client is happy with ads/results
- One referral chain example: 1 client → 4 referrals → 5 clients total → 5× revenue from one outreach

> "Make sure you always ask for referrals — not right away, but 2 weeks after they're satisfied."

## Summary

| Phase | Action | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Niche | Pick high-ticket (construction, plumbing, electrical, remodeling) | Research |
| Lead gen | Cold Instagram DMs or cold calling | Instagram / Google Maps |
| Hook | "I already built you a website" — show mockup in 5 min | Reloom.io / AI |
| Offer | Value stack on call — website + SEO + GMB + ads | Sales call |
| Onboard | Send Jotform onboarding form | Jotform |
| Hosting | Set up Cloudways ($14/month, unlimited sites) | Cloudways + WordPress |

| Recurring | Upsell Google Ads 1–2 weeks after delivery | Google Ads |
| Referrals | Ask 2 weeks after client is satisfied | Direct ask |

**Key numbers:**
- Hosting cost: $14/month (Cloudways, 5 sites)
- Website build cost: ~$85 (Fiverr)
- Minimum website sale price: $500–$1,000
- Ads management: $500–$2,500/month
- Referral multiplier: 1 client → up to 5 clients (real student example)

**Core principle:** Website = door opener. Recurring services (hosting, maintenance, ads) = real business. Delegate build work → stay focused on sales and relationships → scale without ceiling.
