## Overview — The Two-Step System

- No paid ads, no cold calling required
- Entirely runs inside **GoHighLevel (GHL)**
- Two core parts:
  1. **Build a lead list** — find local businesses you can reach
  2. **Clean and filter** — only contact people with highest chance of responding
- Then: reach out at scale via **SMS**, optionally pair with a **power dialer**
- At the end: handle replies so you never leave money on the table

## Step 1 — Building Your Lead List

> Most people do this wrong, which is why their outreach fails completely.

### Free Tool — Chrome Extension (Google Maps Scraper)

1. Install the Chrome extension (free — install from the extension's page)
2. Go to **Google Maps**
3. Search for your target niche — e.g., *landscapers*, *roofers*, *plumbers*
4. Click **"Extract Business Data"** — the extension auto-scrolls and dumps everything into a spreadsheet
5. Google caps results per search (example: 123 companies for Las Vegas landscapers)
6. **Workaround for the cap:**
   - Zoom into a specific area on the map
   - New businesses appear
   - Extract again, add to your master list
   - Repeat across different zones to build hundreds to thousands of leads

### Paid Tool — tryoutscraper.com

- Go to **Services → Google Maps Scraper**
- Select your target area
- **Uncheck irrelevant boxes** (filter precisely for what you want to sell)
- Advanced filter options:
  - Businesses **with** or **without** a website
  - Google verified profiles
  - Good or bad ratings
- Hit **"Get Data"**
- Example: ~4,000 businesses for ~$12
- Hit **Confirm** to download

## Step 2 — Filtering for Cell Phones

> You don't want to text just anybody. You want cell phones — and cell phones usually belong to the business owner directly, not the front desk.

- The scraper tool gives **100 free credits** (test with a list of 100 for free)
- After downloading your list, open it in a spreadsheet
- The list shows phone type: **mobile**, **fixed line**, or **landline**
- **Action:**
  1. Sort by phone type ascending
  2. Select all non-mobile entries (fixed line, landline)
  3. **Delete them**
- Result: a clean list of mobile numbers only
- Example: 1,000+ mobile lines from one scrape session

## Step 4 — SMS Outreach at Scale

### Initial Text Message

> "Hey man, this is Pavel, looking you guys up on Google."

- Simple, casual, non-salesy
- One of the highest-performing messages tested
- The goal is to feel like a real human, not a bot

### Follow-Up Sequence

- Pipeline stages in GHL:
  - **Initial text**
  - **First follow-up**
  - **Second follow-up**
- Leads are moved through pipeline stages automatically
- Notifications fire when a positive reply comes in

### Expected Reply Rate

- **2% to 4% positive reply rate** on SMS
- This is considered **good** in this context
- Example: a lead that didn't respond to the first text replied after **3 follow-ups** and said "Show me the website"

### Handling Non-Interested Leads

- Anyone who says "not interested" → **automatically added to Do Not Disturb**
- They will never receive another text
- This is fully automated inside GHL

### Scaling SMS Volume

- Start: **50 messages/day**
- Target: **100 messages/day**
- To scale beyond: create **multiple GHL sub-accounts**
  - Each sub-account: 100 texts/day
  - Each sub-account is **free** to create inside GHL
  - With 10 sub-accounts: **1,000 texts/day**

## Step 5 — Power Dialer (Calling at Scale)

> Calling every person manually is slow. The power dialer auto-dials one number after another.

**How to set it up in GHL:**

1. Go to **Contacts**, select the leads you want to call (20, all, or any subset)
2. Click **Trigger Automation**
3. Add them to the **Power Dialer Workflow** (already pre-built if using the course template)
4. Hit **Send All at Once** → **Add to Automation** → **Dismiss**
5. Go to **Conversations → Manual Actions**
6. All leads appear — hit **"Let's Start"**
7. GHL automatically dials the first lead
8. After each call, select outcome: **No Answer / Voicemail / Redial / Next**
9. Hitting **Next** immediately dials the next person and pulls up their GHL conversation

**Power Dialer Workflow internals:**

- Creates/updates the opportunity (adds to cold call campaign)
- Tags the contact as **"power dialer"**
- Adds qualifying questions to notes tab for that contact
- Fires the manual call
- Tags the contact **"cold call"** so you track who you've dialed
- Branches based on outcome:
  - Did they book a call? → Yes / No
  - Outcome classification: Interested / Not Interested / Call Back Later
  - Forks into different workflows and pipeline stages

> Note: Even if you skip the power dialer entirely and only call people who replied positively to SMS, you will still see a lot of success.

## The Golden Rule — Always Call, Never SMS-Chat

> Do **not** have a full SMS conversation with any prospect.

- The only acceptable SMS reply after they respond: **"Hey, I just tried to call you."**
- Your **only goal** on SMS is to get them on a phone call
- On the phone call, your **only goal** is to book a demo / Zoom call
- Do not sell over text — sell over voice and video

## The Demo Call — Closing on Zoom

### Booking the Call

- When someone replies positively: **call them immediately**
- If they're ready now, say: "Can you jump on Zoom or Google Meet right now?"
- Some leads will say yes on the spot — be ready to demo immediately

### What to Show on the Zoom Call

1. **Their Google Business Profile** — show them what they currently look like online
2. **Their competitors' websites** — show that competitors all have websites and reviews
3. **Your template website** — built for their industry, even if it's just a template with their logo dropped in

> They will like it because it's better than nothing.

### Getting Website Templates Fast

- **Option 1:** Use presenter's free templates / snapshots (available via his course/community)
- **Option 2:** Use **Smart Cloner** — a tool that clones other websites in the market
  - Paste a competitor's URL
  - It clones the site
  - Drop the client's logo on top as a temporary placeholder
  - After closing, build out a unique, custom version for them
