## Come Prepared (Takes 2 Minutes)

Before the call, think about:

- **What does your business do best?** (your strongest service, the one you want more of)
- **How do most clients find you right now?** (word of mouth, Google, referrals?)

You don't need to prepare anything formal — just have these in the back of your mind. It makes the call faster and more useful for you.
