## Overview

- Offer free website mockups to cold leads as an entry point
- Goal: get them interested → close on a fully published website for thousands of dollars
- Full process per client: ~5 minutes (mockup creation) to 20–30 minutes (with Figma customization)
- Tools used: Reloom.io (free), Figma, Webflow (optional)

## Cold Instagram DM Strategy

### Finding Leads on Instagram

1. Open Instagram → click **Search**
2. Search `[niche] [city]` — e.g., `landscaping Miami` or `landscaping MIA`
3. Browse posts that appear in results
4. Open ~20 posts in new tabs at once
5. For each account:
   - Like 3 of their posts
   - Follow them
   - Send an opening DM

### Warming Up the Lead

**Goal:** get them to reply — just to land in their **main inbox** (not message requests).

**Opening DM example:**
> "Hey I just found your profile and love your work. Do you work in all of Miami?"

- Keep it conversational, no spelling mistakes
- Ask a location/service question — makes you look like a potential customer
- Do this for all 20 accounts

### Pitching Web Design Services

Once they reply, send the pitch. **Voice messages outperform text** (higher answer rate).

**Script (two variations):**

If they have a website but it needs improvement:
> "Hey, I just found your profile and your work looks amazing. I noticed your website could use some improvement. I was curious if you ever thought about upgrading your website. No worries — I already have it fully built out for you. I just want to show it to you if you have time for a 10–15 minute call."

If they have no website:
> "I tried to click a link in your bio and couldn't find a website. I was curious if you ever thought about getting a website. No worries — I already have it fully built out for you. I just want to show it to you if you have time for a 10–15 minute call."

**Key line:** *"I already have it fully built out for you"* — creates urgency and removes friction. Send the call invite or phone number immediately after they show interest.

### Real Client Example — $1,500 Close via DM

- Opened with: *"Hey just found your profile, do you work in all of the GTA?"*
- They replied → sent a voice message → sent phone number immediately
- They texted directly on his phone number: *"Yeah of course, I just want to see the website"*
- Became a paying client — closed at **$1,500** entirely through Instagram DMs

## Building the Website Mockup (Reloom.io)

**Tool:** [reloom.io](https://reloom.io) — free tier available (1 free project at a time)

Total time: **5 minutes** with Reloom, **20–30 minutes** with full Figma customization

### Creating the Project

1. Go to reloom.io → click **Start Free** / **Launch**
2. Free plan: 1 project slot — delete existing project if needed
3. Click **New Project** (top right)
4. Describe the company in a few words
   - Example: `"Abu Landscaping is a landscaping company"`
5. Click **Generate Site Map**

### Generating the Sitemap and Wireframe

1. AI generates full sitemap automatically
2. Click **Wireframe** — AI generates full wireframe including copywriting
3. Wait a few seconds — done

Output includes:
- Full page layout
- Auto-generated copywriting for all sections
- No images by default (add manually)

### Customizing the Wireframe

**Deleting sections:**
- Click section → right-click → Delete, or press `Delete` key
- Example: remove Team section if not needed

**Changing a section's design:**
- Click section → click **Shuffle Component** → AI redesigns it
- Or click the **arrow on the right** to browse alternative hero/section variants

**Changing fonts:**
- Go to **Body** settings → search font name (e.g., `Poppins`) → applies live with preview

**Changing colors:**
- Click **Surprise Me** → randomizes full color scheme and fonts
- Colors are pre-matched for visual consistency
- Use this to make each client's site look unique without rebuilding from scratch

**Style Guide / Tile Guide:**
- Available at the top of Reloom
- Lets you further customize brand colors, button styles, font scales

### Pitch Concepts Feature

- Click **Pitch Concepts** (top right)
- Create multiple design concepts for one client
- Each concept = different look/feel for same site
- Click **Copy Link** → send directly to client
- Client can open and browse all concepts — no export needed
- Replaces the need to export to Figma just for showing the client

## Exporting — Webflow

For clients who want a Webflow site, or if you want to publish directly:

1. In Reloom, select **Webflow** as the export target
2. Click **Clone Style Guide** → creates a Webflow site with all brand colors, fonts, and button styles
3. In Webflow: go to **Apps** (left sidebar) → search `Reloom` → **Install on Site**
4. Log in to Reloom within the Webflow app
5. Select your Reloom project/concept → click **Import** → **Import All Pages**
6. Webflow builds all pages automatically — fully customizable
7. To share with client: click **Share** (top) → **Read Only Link** → copy and send
8. Client can browse the site as a live prototype
9. To publish: click **Publish** → **Add Custom Domain** → buy domain in Webflow

> "I've never used Webflow before but Reloom does everything — all the brand colors, button styles, fonts. You don't need any web design experience."

## Exporting — Figma

### Exporting from Reloom to Figma

1. In Reloom → top right → select **Figma** as export target
2. Click **Export** → enable both export options
3. Click **Open In** → select your Figma file
4. In Figma: go to **Plugins** → search **Reloom** → install (if not already) → click **Run**
5. Log in to Reloom inside Figma
6. Select your wireframe → click **Import Wireframe** → **Start Import**
7. Reloom will report time saved (example: *"You saved 2 hours and 15 minutes"*)

### Customizing in Figma

1. Open a **new Figma file** (keeps things clean)
2. Copy the imported wireframe → paste into new file
3. Delete unwanted elements (e.g., mega menu: double-click to select → delete)
4. Add client images:
   - Source from **Unsplash** (placeholder) or download from client's Instagram
   - Double-click image placeholder → **Add from computer** → upload
5. Add client logo in the same way
6. Update copywriting: change FAQ text, hero copy, section text
7. Match brand colors

> "The better the wireframe, the more interested the client will be."

## Summary

| Step | Action | Tool | Time |
|------|--------|------|------|
| Find leads | Search niche + city | Instagram | — |
| Warm up | Like 3 posts, follow, send opener DM | Instagram | ~2 min/account |
| Pitch | Voice message with "already built" hook | Instagram | — |
| Build mockup | AI sitemap + wireframe | Reloom.io | 2–5 min |
| Customize | Colors, fonts, images, copy | Reloom / Figma | 5–25 min |
| Share | Pitch Concepts link or Figma/Webflow share link | Reloom / Figma / Webflow | 1 min |
| Close | Book 10–15 min call, show mockup | Phone/DM | — |

**Key principles:**
- Lead with value — give the mockup before asking for money
- "I already have it fully built for you" = strongest hook in the pitch
- Voice messages outperform text DMs for response rate
- Reloom automates 90% of the design work — no design skill required
- Use Pitch Concepts to send multiple design variations via a single link
- Client acquisition cost = $0 (pure outreach)
- Closing price demonstrated: $1,500 (GTA client, single Instagram DM thread)
