## Overview

Three proven client acquisition methods for AI web agencies. Each method targets closing clients at **$1,000–$2,000 per website**. No prior experience required.

- All strategies are currently in active use (not theory)
- Choose the method that fits your personality and schedule
- Students have closed $1,000–$2,000 within **2 days** of starting

---

## The Secret Sauce

**Core principle across ALL three methods:**

> "I already built you a website. Do you want to see it?"

Stop asking "Do you need a website?" — that's what every generic agency does.

**Why this works:**
- Business owner thinks: "You already built it? I have nothing to lose."
- Creates curiosity and lowers resistance
- Gets a foot in the door without selling upfront

**Workflow:**
1. Identify prospect
2. Build a quick AI mock-up (Lovable, Reloom, or any AI website builder — takes ~5 minutes)
3. Reach out saying you already built it
4. Book a call to show it

## Method 1: Cold Instagram DMs

**Best for:** Introverts, people working 9-5, anyone with phone anxiety

**Pros:**
- Zero phone anxiety
- Can be done anywhere (at work, in bed)
- High response rate especially with voice messages
- No rejection pressure

**Cons:**
- Slower to scale (starts picking up after 1–2 weeks)
- Requires managing multiple conversations

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1 — Find Target Businesses

1. Go to Instagram
2. Search: `[niche] in [city]` — e.g., "landscaping in Miami" or "construction in Toronto"
3. Filter for **real local business accounts** — avoid large corporations (200k+ followers)
4. Open **20–30 tabs** of prospects

#### Step 2 — Warm Up the Account

- Use your **personal account**, NOT a web design agency account
  - Agency account signals the pitch immediately — kills the approach
- Follow the prospect's account
- Like **2–3 of their posts** — makes you look like a human, not a bot

#### Step 3 — Start the Conversation

Send a **normal question** that makes you look like a potential client:

> "Hey, I just found your profile and love your work. Do you work in all of Miami?"

- This moves you from the **message request** folder into the **main inbox**
- Once they reply, you have a warm channel to pitch

#### Step 4 — Send a Voice Message Pitch

Voice messages outperform text because:
- Tonality comes through — proves you're human, not AI
- Recipient is **forced to listen** (can't skim like text)
- Harder to ignore a 20–30 second audio clip

**Example voice message script:**

> "Hey, I actually just found your profile. The reason I reached out is because I noticed you had super good work, and I didn't find a website in your bio. I thought it was a shame that you had such good work but didn't have anywhere to display the quality of it. So guess what? I just built you a brand new website. If you have 5 minutes, let me just show it to you."

#### Step 5 — Book the Call

- Once they show interest → book a video call
- Build the mock-up if not done yet (AI tools make this ~5 minutes)

### Limits to Avoid Getting Banned

- Max **15 DMs per hour**
- Max **100 DMs per day**

## Method 2: Cold Calling

**Best for:** People who want results **fast**, those willing to push past discomfort

**Pros:**
- Fastest path to revenue — results possible within **hours**
- Real-time conversation = higher conversion per contact
- Immediate yes/no — no waiting around
- Direct access to decision maker (business owner)
- Builds sales skills faster than any other method

**Cons:**
- Requires overcoming phone anxiety
- Must be done during business hours

> "If you need $1,000 in the next 3 days — cold calling is the only answer."

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1 — Find Prospects on Google Maps

1. Go to `maps.google.com`
2. Search: `[niche] in [city]` — e.g., "construction in Miami"
3. Filter for businesses with **under 120 reviews** — sweet spot for reachable owners
4. Skip businesses with too many reviews (larger companies, harder to reach owner)
5. Check if they have a website — adjusts your script

**Speed is everything.** Don't analyze each prospect deeply — scan fast.

> Look at reviews. Look if they have a website. That's it. Move on.

#### Step 2 — Own the Script (Be Yourself)

Don't copy someone else's personality. Be authentic — clients feel it.

**Basic script skeleton:**

> "Hey, is this [Business Name]? Awesome. I just found you guys on Google and noticed you had super good reviews. I clicked on your website and noticed it could use some improvement. So I just went ahead and built you a brand new website — if you got 5 minutes to see it."

**Script variation based on prospect:**

| Situation | Script Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Has a website | "noticed your website could use some improvement" |
| No website | "noticed you didn't have a website — I just made you one" |

#### Step 3 — Handle Objections & Lock In the Meeting

**Goal of the cold call:** NOT to close the sale. Goal = book the second call.

- Getting ghosted is normal — it's a numbers game
- You're not looking for someone who wanted a website already
- You're looking for someone **open to a conversation**

Common objection: "I don't have time."
- Push back lightly if it fits your personality, e.g., "Come on man, just 5 minutes?"
- Or acknowledge and ask to schedule: "No worries — when's a better time?"

#### Step 4 — Build Mock-Up & Run Closing Call

See [Closing the Deal](#closing-the-deal) section below.

## Method 3: Cold Facebook Outreach

**Best for:** People who hate rejection, want inbound leads, dislike both calling and DMs

**Pros:**
- Post once → leads come to you
- No outbound rejection
- Leads arrive within 24–48 hours
- People who message you are already interested

**Cons:**
- Passive — less control over results
- Easy to get banned on Facebook if not careful
- Less predictable than cold calling

> Preference note: If you want inbound leads, running paid ads may be more effective long-term.

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1 — Join the Right Facebook Groups

1. Search Facebook for: `[niche] + [city]` groups
   - e.g., "landscaping Miami", "construction Toronto"
2. Join **10–20 groups** where your target clients hang out
3. Answer group entry questions:
   - "Where are you from?" → Put the local city
   - "Why are you here?" → Something generic like "landscaping services"

> Don't add too many people at once — Facebook bans fast.

#### Step 2 — Drop the Free Mock-Up Offer Post

Post this (copy as-is or adapt):

> "Hey, I just launched my own web design agency and I'm giving away **free website mock-ups** (for businesses in [city/area]). No catch. I'm just trying to build my portfolio and help you guys level up. If you're down, just comment below or hit my DMs."

- This generates **inbound messages** — people who want what you offer
- No cold rejection since they're coming to you

#### Step 3 — Qualify and Gather Info

When someone messages:
- Ask minimal questions (too many = friction = they drop off)
- Key questions:
  - What's your business name?
  - Any color preferences?

> "I'm going to get started on building the website right away."

Build hype around the AI website builder.

#### Step 4 — Turn Free Mock-Up Into Paid Project

1. Build a quick website using AI tools
2. Send them a **screenshot preview**
3. Say: "Let's hop on a quick call — let me run you through it."
4. On the call → close them (see [Closing the Deal](#closing-the-deal))

**Why this converts:**
- They've already seen what you can do before paying
- Zero risk for them — they know what they're getting
- They pay to **publish** the site they've already approved

## Closing the Deal

Applies to all three methods after the prospect has agreed to a call.

### The Closing Call Process

1. **Build the AI mock-up** using Lovable, Reloom, or equivalent — takes ~5 minutes
2. **Walk them through it** on a Zoom/video call
3. **Show the comparison:**
   - Pull up their old (or non-existent) website
   - Show your mock-up side by side
   - Explain what makes yours better
4. **Build the gap** — increase perceived value of having a quality website
5. **Present the price** — $1,000–$2,000
6. **Close and launch**

### Key Sales Principle

> "Build the gap between where they are and where they could be. Then present the price."

The prospect already said yes to seeing it. They're warm. The close is about helping them see the value, not hard selling.

## Summary & Method Comparison

| Method | Best For | Speed to Results | Effort | Rejection Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold Instagram DMs** | Introverts, 9-5 workers | 1–2 weeks | Low | Low |
| **Cold Calling** | Fast results seekers | Hours to days | High | High |
| **Facebook Outreach** | Hate sales, want inbound | 24–48 hours | Very Low | None |

### Pricing Targets

- Entry-level close: **$1,000**
- Mid-level close: **$1,500–$2,000**

### Tools Mentioned

- **Lovable** — AI website builder for fast mock-ups
- **Reloom** — Alternative AI website builder
- **Google Maps** — Prospect research for cold calling
- **Instagram** — DM outreach
- **Facebook Groups** — Inbound post strategy
- **Zoom** — Closing calls

### Key Numbers to Hit

| Activity | Daily Target |
|---|---|
| Instagram DMs | Up to 100/day (max 15/hour) |
| Cold calls | As many as possible during business hours |
| Facebook group posts | 1 post per group (don't spam) |
| Google Maps prospects | Fast scan — don't overthink |

### Final Takeaway

> Pick ONE method. Go all in on numbers. The secret sauce (pre-built mock-up) stays the same across all three. Volume beats perfection every time.
