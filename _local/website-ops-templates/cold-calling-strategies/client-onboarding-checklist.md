# Client Onboarding Checklist

Complete this before touching Reloom/Vercel. Missing info = delays = bad impression.

---

## Step 1 — Collect Payment

- [ ] e-Transfer received (setup fee)
- [ ] Send payment confirmation message to client

---

## Step 2 — Business Info

- [ ] Legal business name
- [ ] Tagline or slogan (if they have one)
- [ ] Business phone number
- [ ] Business email address
- [ ] Physical address (or service area if mobile)
- [ ] Years in business
- [ ] Services offered (full list)
- [ ] Service areas / cities covered

---

## Step 3 — Branding Assets

- [ ] Logo file (PNG with transparent background preferred)
- [ ] Brand colors (hex codes or just ask "what colors do you like?")
- [ ] Photos of their work (min 5–10 good ones)
- [ ] Photos of the team or owner (optional but builds trust)
- [ ] Any existing marketing material (flyers, business cards)

> If no logo exists — offer basic logo for extra fee or use their business name as text.
> If no photos — use Unsplash placeholders, swap later.

---

## Step 4 — Website Content

- [ ] About section: how long in business, what makes them different, who they serve
- [ ] Services: name + short description for each service
- [ ] FAQ: ask them "what questions do customers ask you most?"
- [ ] Testimonials: ask for 2–3 client reviews (Google reviews work too)
- [ ] Call to action preference: "Call us", "Get a free quote", "Book online"?

---

## Step 5 — Domain & Hosting

- [ ] Do they have an existing domain? (yes/no)
  - If yes → get login credentials or ask them to add you as admin
  - If no → you buy it (add $15–20 to invoice) or they buy on Hostinger
- [ ] Confirm hosting is covered under their monthly plan (your Vercel account)

---

## Step 6 — Integrations

- [ ] Contact form email — where should leads go? (their business email)
- [ ] Google Maps listing link (for embedding)
- [ ] Google Analytics — do they want tracking? (set up for them)
- [ ] Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
- [ ] Booking system needed? (yes/no — upsell if yes)

---

## Step 7 — Set Expectations

Tell client clearly:

- [ ] Timeline: "Your site will be ready in X business days"
- [ ] Review process: "I'll send you a preview link — give me feedback within 48 hours"
- [ ] Revision policy: "Includes X rounds of revisions"
- [ ] Go-live: "Once you approve, I publish within 24 hours"
- [ ] Monthly retainer starts: first payment date

---

## Step 8 — After Launch

- [ ] Send client the live website link
- [ ] Confirm contact form is working (test it yourself)
- [ ] Confirm site is mobile responsive (check on phone)
- [ ] Submit site to Google Search Console (optional but good)
- [ ] Ask for Google review / testimonial
- [ ] Schedule first monthly check-in

---

## Quick Message Template (send after payment)

> "Hey [Name], payment received — thanks! To get started on your website, I just need a few things from you:
> 1. Your logo (PNG if you have it)
> 2. 5–10 photos of your work
> 3. A list of your services
> 4. The email address where you want leads sent
>
> Once I have these, I'll have a preview ready for you within [X] days."

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't start building before payment clears
- Don't ask for everything at once in one long message — overwhelms client
- Don't promise SEO ranking results — only promise a professional site
- Don't give them hosting/Vercel login — you manage it, they pay monthly
