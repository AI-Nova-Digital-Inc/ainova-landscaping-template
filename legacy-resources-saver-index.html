Frequently Asked Questions About Our Landscaping Services

In most Waterloo Region municipalities, a building permit is required if your stone retaining wall exceeds 1 meter (3 feet 3 inches) in height or is adjacent to a driveway or property line. Our experienced team handles all structural engineering and permit applications to ensure your landscape project is fully compliant and safe.

The ideal time for new sod installation in Ontario is during the cooler, wetter months of early spring or early fall. However, our premium Kentucky Bluegrass sod can be successfully laid throughout the summer as long as a strict, consistent watering schedule is maintained for the first three weeks to establish deep roots.

Interlock driveways use individual high-density paving stones, making them incredibly durable and resistant to the freeze-thaw cycles of Kitchener winters. Unlike poured concrete, which inevitably cracks and is costly to repair, interlock pavers flex with ground shifts and allow for easy, localized repairs by simply replacing a single stone.
